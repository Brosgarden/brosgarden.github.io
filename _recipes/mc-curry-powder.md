## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Cumin seeds | 30 g |
| Coriander seeds | 20 g |
| Black peppercorns | 20 g |
| Cinnamon stick, broken into small pieces | 2.7 g |
| Cloves | 2 g |
| Kashmiri chilies (optional) | 1 g |
| Mace blades, broken into small pieces | 1 g |
| Green cardamom seeds | 0.5 g |

## Equipment

- Baking sheet
- Coffee grinder or dedicated spice grinder
- Airtight storage container or vacuum sealer

## Method

1. **Preheat oven:** Preheat oven to 340°F.

2. **Combine spices:** Combine cumin seeds, coriander seeds, black peppercorns, cinnamon, cloves, Kashmiri chilies (if using), mace, and cardamom seeds on a baking sheet.

3. **Toast:** Spread spices in an even layer. Toast in the oven for 8–10 minutes, until golden and aromatic. Watch carefully to prevent scorching.

4. **Cool:** Remove from the oven and allow the spices to cool completely.

5. **Grind:** Transfer the toasted spices to a coffee grinder or spice grinder. Grind to a fine, uniform powder.

6. **Store:** Transfer to an airtight container.

## Storage

- Airtight container: Up to 2 weeks.
- Vacuum sealed and refrigerated: Up to 2 months.

For best aroma retention, vacuum seal the spice blend to protect its volatile oils and prevent moisture absorption.

## Variation: Vindaloo Spice Mix

Double the black peppercorns to 40 g while keeping the remaining ingredients unchanged.

## Notes

- Toasting whole spices in the oven provides more even heat and reduces the risk of scorching compared with dry-toasting in a skillet.
- Use a dedicated coffee grinder for spices to avoid transferring coffee aromas into the blend.
- If Kashmiri chilies are unavailable, substitute 2 g sweet paprika after toasting and grinding the other spices. Do not toast powdered paprika.
- This blend is suitable for curry sauces, marinades, meat preparations, and general-purpose seasoning.
